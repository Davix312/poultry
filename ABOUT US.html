About PoultryLink Uganda

PoultryLink Uganda is a proposed digital platform designed to make poultry products easier to market and find.

The platform aims to bring poultry farmers, buyers, traders, restaurants and other poultry stakeholders together in one convenient online space.

Our Mission

To connect poultry farmers with buyers through an organized online platform.

Our Vision

To promote easier access to poultry markets and useful poultry information through ICT.

Our Objectives

  1. Support farmers in marketing poultry products.
  2. Connect farmers with potential buyers.
  3. Provide poultry market information.
  4. Organize poultry products into categories.
  5. Provide useful poultry farming tips.

How PoultryLink Works

Farmers can present their poultry products and provide information about their available stock.

Buyers can browse products, compare available options and contact sellers.

This makes PoultryLink Uganda a simple bridge between farmers and poultry buyers.
