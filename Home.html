Poultry Farming Tips

1. Proper Feeding

Provide balanced poultry feed appropriate for the birds' age, purpose and stage of growth. Ensure clean drinking water is always available.

2. Clean Housing

Keep poultry houses clean, dry and well ventilated. Regular cleaning helps reduce the spread of diseases.

3. Disease Prevention

Follow appropriate vaccination and biosecurity practices. Consult a qualified veterinary professional when birds show signs of illness.

4. Good Record Keeping

Record feed costs, egg production, sales, mortality and other expenses to help monitor the performance of your poultry business.
