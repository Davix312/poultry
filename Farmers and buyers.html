Farmers and Buyers

PoultryLink Uganda connects poultry farmers with customers looking for poultry products.

Farmers can present their products while buyers can discover products and contact suitable sellers.

How Farmers and Buyers Connect

Farmers provide information about their poultry products.

Buyers browse available products and identify suitable sellers.

Buyers can then contact farmers for further information and purchasing.
